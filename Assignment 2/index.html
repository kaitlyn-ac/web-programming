About Me

I'm someone who thrives on reinvention and deliberate challenge — after earning a Bachelor of Commerce, I built a strong foundation in the financial sector and later supported operations at an engineering firm. However, I knew I wanted to become a true technical expert capable of solving deeper, more complex problems. That drive led me to pursue Computer Engineering Technology - Computing Science at Algonquin College. Where three semesters of hands-on learning opened the door to a year-long Site Reliability Engineering co-op at Kinaxis. The experience solidified my passion for infrastructure, automation, and problem-solving at scale. Afterwards I recognized that online learning would better support my life and goals. As such, I am now working toward completing a Computer Programming diploma.

Outside of work and school, I intentionally disconnect and immerse myself in hobbies that have nothing to do with tech. Even though they don't always align with stereotypical “tech culture,” I'm encouraged by the growing number of women entering the industry and feel increasingly confident that there's space for people like me to belong without having to fit a mold.
